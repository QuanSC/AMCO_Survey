AMCO Member Survey 2022–2026

Interactive data dashboard for the Academic Medical Center Optometrists (AMCO) SIG longitudinal member survey, presented at the American Academy of Optometry Annual Meeting 2026.

Live site: QuanSC.github.io/AMCO_Survey

What this is

A self-contained single-page web app presenting three cycles of survey data (2022, 2024, 2026) on the working conditions, compensation, academic roles, and professional identity of optometrists employed at U.S. Academic Medical Centers. No server, no build step, no dependencies — just one HTML file.

The page has two sections:

1. Summary Dashboard — narrative findings with headline stats, trend charts, and key takeaways from all three cycles. Designed for a conference audience: quick to scan, data-dense, mobile-friendly.

2. Per-Cycle Explorer — a filterable, interactive tool that lets you slice the 2022 and 2024 individual respondent data by region and tenure, and compare against 2026 aggregate figures across four topic tabs: Compensation, Research, Identity, and OMD Relations.

Key findings (2022–2026)
Compensation surged: the >$200K earner band grew from ~16% (2022) to 40% (2026)
Research time nearly gone: serious FTE (≥25% protected time) collapsed from 13.3% → 3.9%
Collegial equality with OMDs dipped: "equals" fell from 69% (2024) back to 57% (2026), below the 2022 baseline
Academic advancement as a motivator tripled (35.4 → 47.4), even as structural support eroded
73% lecture OMD residents; 92% attend Grand Rounds — largely uncompensated relative to MD peers
